Shizora Tools Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Shizora Tools ("the app") is a cosmetic customization tool. This policy explains what the app does with data.

Data we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or phone number.

Network requests

The app downloads its catalog, images, and content packs from public servers (GitHub and its CDN). These servers may log standard technical information such as your IP address, as any website does. We do not receive or store this information.

On-device data

Your settings, favorites, and applied items are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

Internet access is used to download content. Shizuku access or a folder permission you grant is used only to copy content packs into the folder you choose. Storage permission is requested only on Android 10 and below for the same purpose.

Third parties

The app has no ads, no analytics, and does not sell or share data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
